WHO WE ARE

A community network built around practical solutions.

Ssembabule Community Action Network (SCAN) is a proposed non-profit community development organisation based in Ssembabule District, Uganda.

We aim to connect communities, local institutions, volunteers and development partners to identify priorities and turn community ideas into practical action.

“A stronger community is built when people participate in shaping its future.”
OUR FOUNDATION — ARTICLES 2-5

A resilient, empowered and prosperous community where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

Vision — Article 3

We, the founding members, recognising the importance of community participation, social inclusion, sustainable livelihoods, education, health and economic empowerment, hereby establish this organisation to contribute to sustainable development and wellbeing.

“Connecting Communities. Creating Change.” — Article 5 Tagline

Our Mission — Article 4

To mobilise and empower communities through sustainable development initiatives that improve livelihoods, health, education and social wellbeing.

Nature of Organisation — Article 2

SCAN is a non-profit, non-political and non-sectarian organisation operating for public benefit in accordance with the laws of Uganda. No income or property shall be distributed to members for personal benefit. Initial operations in Ssembabule District.

Our Principles — Preamble

Integrity • Accountability • Transparency • Equality • Inclusion • Community Participation • Respect for Human Dignity

ARTICLES 11-24 — GOVERNANCE & FINANCIAL INTEGRITY

How we are governed

As defined in our Constitution, SCAN is guided by a transparent governance structure to ensure accountability.

General Assembly

Supreme Body — Article 12

Approves major policies, strategic plans, financial reports, and Constitutional amendments. Quorum: 1/2 of voting members. Dissolution requires 2/3.

Board of Directors

Oversight — Articles 13-17

Chairperson, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer + 2 Members. Provides strategic direction, financial oversight, and appoints Executive Director. Chair provides leadership, Secretary keeps records, Treasurer monitors finances.

Executive Director

Management — Article 18

Day-to-day management, implementing Board decisions, supervising programmes and staff, coordinating partnerships, and resource mobilisation.

Financial Management — Articles 21-24: Bank account in registered name, funds used only for approved purposes, asset register maintained, annual budgets approved, independent audit as required by law/donors, strict conflict of interest policy. No member shall personally own organisational property. Remaining assets on dissolution transferred to similar non-profit per Ugandan law (Article 29).
